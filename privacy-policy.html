Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2025

BibleVibe ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the BibleVibe mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

BibleVibe stores all personal preferences and data locally on your device. This includes:

  • Your display name (optional, set by you)
  • Theme preferences and app icon selection
  • Bible version and language preferences
  • Favorites, bookmarks, and gratitude journal entries
  • Streak and daily progress data
  • Notification/reminder settings
  • Widget configuration preferences
  • Profile photo (stored only on your device)

This data is NOT transmitted to our servers. It remains entirely on your device.

1.2 Subscription Information

If you subscribe to BibleVibe Premium, your purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store. We use RevenueCat, a third-party service, to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID
  • Purchase receipts and subscription status

We do NOT receive or store your payment details (credit card numbers, billing address, etc.). All payment processing is handled by Apple.

1.3 Friends & Widget Features

When you use the Friends Widget feature to share verses with friends, minimal data (verse content, sender identifier) may be exchanged through our backend service to deliver verses to your friends' widgets. No personal messages, location data, or contact information is collected or shared.

1.4 Analytics & Tracking

BibleVibe does NOT use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. We do not track your behavior, location, or browsing activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the locally stored information solely to:

  • Provide and personalize your app experience
  • Display your selected themes, verses, and widgets
  • Manage your subscription status
  • Deliver notification reminders you have configured
  • Enable the Friends Widget verse-sharing feature

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Information is shared only with:

  • Apple: For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • RevenueCat: For subscription management (anonymous data only)

4. Data Security

Your data is stored locally on your device and protected by your device's built-in security features (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID). We implement industry-standard security measures for any data that passes through our services.

5. Children's Privacy

BibleVibe is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Since all data is stored locally on the device, no personal data is transmitted to us.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

All user data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Deleting the BibleVibe app from your device
  • Resetting your profile within the app settings

If you cancel your subscription, your premium features will remain active until the end of the current billing period. Subscription management and cancellation is handled through Apple's App Store settings.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all your data (it's stored locally on your device)
  • Delete your data by removing the app
  • Opt out of notifications at any time through your device settings
  • Cancel your subscription through Apple's App Store

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
