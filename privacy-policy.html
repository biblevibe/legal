Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

BibleVibe ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the BibleVibe mobile application and its home screen widgets (the "App") collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following stays on your device and is not sent to our servers:

  • Theme, app icon, font and widget preferences
  • Bible version and language preferences
  • Favorites, bookmarks and gratitude journal entries
  • Streak and daily progress data
  • Notification and reminder settings

2. Information We Collect and Store on Our Servers

To run the Friends features (adding friends, sending verses and messages to their widgets), the App creates an account for you on our backend service (Supabase). You do not need to sign in for this: the App creates an anonymous account in the background. We store:

  • Account identifiers: a random account ID and your friend code.
  • Display name: the name you enter in the App. Other users who know your friend code can see it.
  • Profile photo: if you add one, it is uploaded to our storage so your friends can see it. Anyone who has the photo's link can view it.
  • Friends and friend requests: which friend codes you are connected to, and requests you send or receive.
  • Content you send: verses, quotes, prayers, affirmations and custom messages you send to friends, with the time sent and how long they stay on the widget. Only you and the recipient can read them.
  • Push notification token: a device token used to notify your friends when you send them something, and to notify you. Only our server can read it.
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple account identifier and the email address Apple shares with us. This may be a private relay address if you choose "Hide My Email."
  • Feedback: if you send feedback, we store your message with your friend code.
  • Reports and blocks: if you report or block someone, we store the report, including your reason and that person's latest message to you, and the block. We use reports only to review and act on content that breaks our rules.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following service providers to operate the App. They process data on our behalf and under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase: hosts our database, file storage and server functions for the data listed in Section 2.
  • Google Firebase:
    • Cloud Messaging: delivers push notifications.
    • Remote Config: delivers content updates.
    • Analytics: collects app usage information, such as app opens, sessions, device model, OS version and an app instance identifier, to help us understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions. It receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts and your subscription status.
  • Apple: processes in-app purchases and, if you use it, Sign in with Apple. We never receive your payment card details.
  • FormSubmit: when you send feedback, it forwards your message, friend code and, if you signed in with Apple, your email address to our support inbox.

We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Friends features: find friends by code, deliver verses and messages to their widgets, and send notifications
  • To manage your subscription and Premium features
  • To respond to your feedback and support requests
  • To understand overall usage and improve the App
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse

5. Data Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access rules on our servers let each account read and change only its own data and the messages it sends or receives. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your server data while your account exists. Messages you send expire from your friend's widget after the duration you choose. They remain in your shared history until you or your friend deletes the account.

You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes:

  • Your profile, friend connections and friend requests
  • Messages you sent and received
  • Your feedback
  • Your push token
  • Your server account

Deleting the App from your device removes the data stored only on your device. It does not delete your server account. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us (see Section 10).

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the EU/UK GDPR, Turkey's KVKK and US state privacy laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct or delete your personal information
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Receive a copy of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with your data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email us. You can turn off notifications at any time in your device settings.

8. International Transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the providers' standard contractual clauses.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy
